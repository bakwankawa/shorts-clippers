Privacy policy

Shorts clippers · last updated 23 November 2026

Who uses it

Shorts clippers is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google accounts and YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else and collects no information about other people.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, it uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set their visibility, and read the owner's channel and video statistics (views, likes, watch time and estimated revenue) from the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API.

Where the data stays

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the program's data folder removes everything it stored.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
